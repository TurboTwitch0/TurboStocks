    ON-DEVICE MARKET TUTOR

    Your market study partner

    The built-in study guide works without downloads. Optional Qwen AI generates text on this device after a model download. Current-news requests may show linked Google News excerpts; paper values use fixed educational samples.

    Turbo Tutor

    I’m Turbo Tutor. Ask about a market concept, or type “quiz me.” Answers are labelled as built-in guidance, local-model text, retrieved news, or calculated paper values.

    OCR reads visible text only; it does not identify candle shapes.

    Study tools

    Built-in tutor ready. Qwen on-device AI needs WebGPU, about 1 GB of GPU memory, and a one-time model download.

    No API key or credits. First use needs a model download, WebGPU, HTTPS/localhost and sufficient GPU memory. Reload before changing a loaded model.

    Opens ChatGPT separately and copies your draft prompt. Upload chart images there manually; nothing is sent automatically. ChatGPT sign-in and plan limits are separate.

    The model runs on your device after download. It has no live market feed and is for education, not buy/sell recommendations.
